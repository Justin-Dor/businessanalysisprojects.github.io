Unilever Smart Canteen Ordering System (USCOS)
(Enterprise Project)

A strategic digital transformation initiative that modernises the end-to-end employee meal ordering process by replacing fragmented manual activities with a secure, integrated, and automated enterprise platform. The project applied structured business analysis and investment appraisal techniques including stakeholder engagement, requirements elicitation, SWOT, PESTLE, Weighted Options Assessment, Cost-Benefit Analysis, and Financial Evaluation (ROI, NPV, IRR, and Payback) to identify the preferred solution and support evidence-based investment decisions. Comprehensive end-to-end traceability was established from business objectives through business Capabilities, Requirements, Business Rules, User Stories, BPMN Processes, and Benefits, ensuring Robust Governance, Auditability, and Controlled delivery. USCOS is expected to Enhance Employee Experience, Reduce Manual Effort, Minimise Food Wastage, Automate Payroll Deductions, Strengthen Compliance and Reporting, and Deliver Measurable Operational Efficiencies and Long-Term business value aligned with Unilever's Digital Transformation objectives.

Synergy Enterprises
(HR Process Optimization)

Synergy Enterprises' HR function runs on a fragmented, organically grown patchwork of regional systems that no longer scales for a 75,000-employee, five-continent FMCG business. We therefore recommend investing £60M in a single Global HCM Platform, delivered through a Hybrid Agile-Waterfall programme, forecast to generate £17.5M in recurring annual benefits, £9.9M NPV, approximately 14% IRR, and payback within 3.4 years. This recommendation is supported by a weighted options appraisal, scenario-based DCF investment modelling, the Five Case Model, and stage-gated Board governance, ensuring it is strategically aligned, financially robust, affordable, and deliverable. Every recommendation is fully traceable through BACCM, a Strategic Alignment & Traceability Matrix, and a Benefits Dependency Network, providing Finance, the PMO, and the Benefits Realisation Board with a complete, auditable line from business need through platform capability to operational, financial, and strategic value. The transformation will deliver a single global employee record, standardised HR processes, enterprise analytics, and employee self-service while strengthening compliance, improving workforce retention, and increasing organisational agility—resolving the root cause of HR fragmentation rather than merely addressing its symptoms.

BikeGear Solutions
(Executive Sales Analytics)

This project transforms six years (2011–2016) of transactional sales data into a single-source executive dashboard that converts £4.83 million of historical sales into actionable business intelligence. Hierarchical KPI decomposition, five-season demand classification, profitability analysis, country and product ranking, and customer demographic segmentation provide a robust evidence base for executive decision-making, while complete data lineage ensures every KPI is fully traceable from the source transactions through financial and seasonal pivot models to the final dashboard. The solution equips leadership to rapidly identify seasonal demand gaps, prioritise high-margin products and high-value customer segments, optimise inventory and marketing investment, and make faster, data-driven strategic decisions.

Release Check
(AI Vehicle Release Validation App)

A self-initiated proof of concept that tackles wrong-vehicle handovers at a vehicle logistics yard, where collections are checked by hand against a printed Daily Diary. I took the idea from a one-page solution outline through Discovery and Gap Analysis (stale diaries, unplated vehicles, OCR misreads, collector verification), Functional and Non-Functional Requirements, safeguards and exception handling, an interactive prototype and a 12-screen clickable mockup, to a working installable phone web app. The operator photographs the diary (up to 3 pages) and the app extracts only collection time and registration on the device; each vehicle's plate is then scanned for a clear OK TO RELEASE, DO NOT RELEASE, MANUAL CHECK or ALREADY RELEASED decision, with collector verification, supervisor override and an exportable audit log. A UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018 review drove privacy-by-design choices: data minimisation, operator IDs instead of names, and configurable plate-photo retention. Built and tested with dummy data only.
