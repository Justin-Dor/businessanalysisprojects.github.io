Synergy Enterprises
(Global HR Transformation)

Synergy Enterprises' HR function ran on a fragmented patchwork of regional systems that no longer scaled for a 75,000-employee FMCG business operating across five continents. I built the business case for investing £60M in a single global HCM platform, delivered through a hybrid Agile-Waterfall programme and forecast to generate £17.5M in recurring annual benefits, a £9.9M NPV, approximately 14% IRR and payback within 3.4 years. The recommendation is supported by a weighted options appraisal, scenario-based DCF investment modelling, the Five Case Model and stage-gated board governance, and is fully traceable through BACCM, a strategic alignment and traceability matrix and a benefits dependency network, giving Finance, the PMO and the Benefits Realisation Board an auditable line from business need to value. The programme delivers a single global employee record, standardised HR processes, enterprise analytics and employee self-service, resolving the root cause of HR fragmentation rather than its symptoms.

Release Check
(AI Vehicle Release Validation App)

An initiative I originated and led from problem to working product, addressing the risk of wrong-vehicle handovers at a vehicle logistics yard where collections were checked by hand against a printed Daily Diary. I took the concept through discovery and gap analysis (stale diaries, unplated vehicles, OCR misreads and collector verification), functional and non-functional requirements, safeguards and exception handling, an interactive prototype and a 12-screen clickable mockup, to a working installable phone web app. The operator photographs the diary (up to three pages) and the app extracts only collection time and registration on the device; each vehicle's plate is then scanned for a clear OK TO RELEASE, DO NOT RELEASE, MANUAL CHECK or ALREADY RELEASED decision, with collector verification, supervisor override and an exportable audit log. A UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 review shaped privacy-by-design choices: data minimisation, operator IDs instead of names and configurable plate-photo retention. Validated end to end with test data, ready for a live pilot subject to organisational approval.

Northbridge Financial Services
(Legacy System & Enterprise Data Migration)

Planned the retirement of Northbridge Financial Services' green-screen mainframe within about nine months and the migration of Customer, Loan and Transaction history to a cloud ERP that is already live, without interrupting live banking. I profiled the source extracts, documented data quality findings and a cleansing sequence, built a source-to-target map, and scored migration options to recommend a phased load with parallel checks over a big-bang cut-over, keeping the mainframe as the book of record until each stage is accepted. Supporting deliverables include the Project Initiation Document, a stakeholder questionnaire, a validation and reconciliation risk register, stage gates, a communication plan and change and training recommendations, with data exceptions escalated to named owners for resolution rather than fixed automatically. HealixOne CareFlow
(Onboarding Experience Transformation)

As a Senior Business Analyst in a multi-analyst team, I redesigned how new customers of HealixOne CareFlow, a healthcare SaaS care-coordination platform, move from a closed sale to a live, adopted implementation. Onboarding took a median 8–10 weeks with no standard trigger, no visible progress and no analytics. Through stakeholder interviews, a Target Operating Model design workshop covering 15 design gaps, journey mapping and Fishbone and 5 Whys root cause analysis, we traced the main delay to an unvalidated CRM handover. Deliverables include a traceable Requirements Catalogue (BR → CR → FR → Epic), a TO-BE Validation Workbook, priority epics and user stories, a Future State process definition with BPMN 2.0 maps, RACI, a KPI framework and low-fidelity wireframes, targeting a 4-week onboarding lead time across a two-release roadmap.
